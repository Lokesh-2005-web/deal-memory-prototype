Title: Project Heron lessons learned (June 2022 acquisition)

Project Heron was a small healthcare software company of about 40 people that we acquired in June 2022.

Moving customer records into the parent company's platform took 4 months longer than planned because of inconsistent record formats.

Staged equity vesting over 24 months kept all 6 core engineers through the first two years.

The parent's security review of the acquired product added 2 months before it could be sold to existing customers.
