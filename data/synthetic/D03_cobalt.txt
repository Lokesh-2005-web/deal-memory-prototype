Title: Project Cobalt post-mortem (September 2021 acquisition)

Project Cobalt was the largest software acquisition we completed in 2021, a retail technology company with about 900 employees.

Unifying login across the two products took 8 months, far beyond the 3 months planned, mostly because of incompatible identity providers.

Retention payments were made up front, and a third of the acquired product team left within a year.

Overlapping customers cancelled one of the two contracts, and revenue synergies came in well below the deal model.

No one owned integration decisions for the first 3 months, which stalled the roadmap merge.
