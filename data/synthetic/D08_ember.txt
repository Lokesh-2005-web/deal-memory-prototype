Title: Project Ember review (January 2022 acquisition)

Project Ember was a large healthcare services group with about 1,200 staff that we acquired in January 2022.

Merging the ERP systems took 7 months longer than planned because the two finance teams used different cost centres.

The steering committee met monthly but could not resolve decision-rights disputes between the two finance teams, adding 2 months.

Overall the integration saw minor delays in some workstreams.
